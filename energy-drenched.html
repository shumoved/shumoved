Shumoved - Energy Drenched

tags: 2016, energy, overdrive, electronic music


This track is highly unconventional and sounds completely different from most other Shumoved compositions. The extremely overdriven sound of a roaring synthesizer, with its pulsating rhythms and melody, instantly grabs the listener’s attention. The track was originally conceived as something that could slowly but powerfully get an entire stadium crowd moving..

(The track was recorded in 2016)
