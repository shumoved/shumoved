Shumoved

Shumoved is an electronic music project that has been active since 1999. Its sound explores rhythm, atmosphere, radio noise, found sounds, and melodic fragments. Some tracks lean towards deep house and chill, while others venture into experimental electronic music and sonic collages.


Until 2025, Shumoved was the solo project of one artist, Dmitry Anufriev. Since then, it has evolved into a record label. Today, the Lapa Sound project is also part of the Shumoved universe.
