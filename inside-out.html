Shumoved - Inside Out

tags: 2026, deep, chill, electronic sound






Before humanity inhabited our planet, there were no weapons, no gasoline, no nuclear power plants, no synthetic drugs, and none of the modern conveniences of civilization we have today: the Internet, electric cars, airplanes, spacecraft and satellites, skyscrapers, microwave ovens, television, and many other things invented by humankind.

The question of whether humanity is developing in the right direction is probably on the minds of many people.

Of course, it would be a shame to disregard the achievements of science and technology. However, using them against other human beings is most likely the wrong way forward.

In recent years, green and clean energy has been developing, although nowhere near as rapidly as we might hope. Its progress is held back by the interests of those who profit from traditional energy sources (nuclear power, for example), as well as by the short-sighted perspectives of some of the people governing our countries.

At the same time, green energy is environmentally friendly and, in a sense, can be said to care more about us, human beings, and the planet we live on.

There are also experimental developments involving fuel-free technologies for generating electricity. And without electricity, practically nothing in our modern world would work: no smartphones, no computers, no lights in our homes, no Internet, no refrigerator in the kitchen, and so on.

Groups of people compete for resources, territory, and status. Sometimes this struggle becomes brutal, involving weapons and human casualties on a horrifying scale.

If everyone suddenly realized that it was time to change the way we live — a way of life that has, in many respects, turned inside out because of these struggles — and began striving for a world in which every person born and living on this planet could enjoy all the necessities of life, with financial resources distributed proportionally among the population, things would surely become more peaceful. There would be more shared joy in knowing that humanity had crossed this threshold and entered a new era.

The weapons used to kill people in wars, weapons manufactured in many countries around the world, could instead be put to a different purpose. Rather than being directed against one another, countries could coordinate their efforts so that, should extraterrestrial beings ever pose a threat to us, humanity would be able to defend itself together, with the combined strength of all nations.

Of course, there are important questions to consider when it comes to distributing the planet's available resources and benefits more equally. One of them is how to manage population growth responsibly, so that we do not exceed the planet's capacity and make life overcrowded and difficult for everyone. Another is how to respect and take into account the traditions of each individual region.

In such a world, people would have an unconditional basic income, poverty would be overcome, and the overall level of human happiness would rise. It could become a new, peaceful chapter in the history of our planet, our Earth.

The instrumental track Inside Out has no lyrics. It is dedicated to the feeling that it is time to rethink our approaches to the realities of today's world (2026).

We are exploring outer space without asking permission from anyone on the other side of the galaxy. But who knows — perhaps UFOs are already heading in our direction, preparing to "explore" our planet using weapons beyond anything we can imagine.

It is time to become friendlier towards one another and remember the principle of good neighbourliness, which has long been enshrined in the UN Charter. In theory, it would be wonderful to see this principle applied not only to relations between nations but also to everyday human interactions.

You may also experience the energy that the artist has tried to convey through the sound of this track.
